# Reviews

**Rule.** Reviews follow `docs/REVIEW-BRIEF.md`: read-only, scoped, in its output format. Reports live in `docs/reviews/`, are kept permanently and are annotated with what happened to each finding.

**Why.** A reviewer that starts fixing stops looking; an unannotated report gets re-litigated from scratch.

**How.**
- Reviewing a pull request before merging it is required (`git-commit-push.md`).
